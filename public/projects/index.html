Notes from the process

Build for the work, not just the technology.

These are guiding ideas behind my experiments, rather than published case studies or product claims.

Start with the real question

For hotel teams, it might be “Where is the approved procedure?” For managers, it might be “What still needs my attention?” The problem shapes the product.

Keep people in control

AI can help organise information and prepare useful suggestions. Human judgement, review and accountability still matter.

Learn by testing small

Prototypes and demos help uncover what is useful before promising a finished solution.

Work together

Have a workflow worth improving?

I work with businesses on remote support, coordination and practical ways to use modern tools.
